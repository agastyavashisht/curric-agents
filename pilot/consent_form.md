## Purpose of the Study

This study evaluates an AI-assisted adaptive learning system. The system uses multiple AI agents to plan a personalised curriculum, generate learning content, assess your understanding through questions, and adapt the sequence based on your performance.

## What You Will Be Asked to Do

- Complete a **pre-test** (10 multiple-choice questions, ~15 minutes) at the start of the study period
- Use the **AI learning system** for approximately **3–5 sessions** over 5 weeks, each session ~20–40 minutes
- Complete a **post-test** (10 multiple-choice questions, ~15 minutes) at the end of the study period
- Total estimated time commitment: **3–5 hours over 5 weeks**

## What Data Is Collected

| Data | Purpose | Stored as |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Your answers to pre/post-test questions | Measuring learning gain | Anonymised score only |
| Your answers to in-session questions | Adapting the curriculum | Anonymised, linked to your study ID |
| Time spent per question | System evaluation | Aggregated, anonymised |
| Agent interaction logs | Technical evaluation of the AI system | No personal data — topic IDs and scores only |

**What is NOT collected:** your name, email, student registration number, demographic information, or any data outside the learning system.

## Anonymisation

You will be assigned an anonymised **Study ID** (e.g., `PILOT-07`). Your real name and Study ID are recorded in a separate mapping file (`student_id_map.csv`) held only by the lead researcher and never uploaded to any system, shared with external parties, or included in the research paper.

All data processed by the AI system uses only your Study ID — your real identity never enters the AI pipeline.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any academic penalty. If you withdraw, your data will be deleted from the study dataset.

## Risks

This study poses minimal risk. The AI system is a prototype and may occasionally produce incorrect or incomplete content. You should not rely on it as your sole learning resource for any assessed coursework.

## Benefits

You will receive early access to an adaptive AI learning tool covering topics in your curriculum. Your participation directly contributes to research on AI in education.

## Data Storage and Retention

- Session data is stored on a password-protected server accessible only to the research team
- All data will be retained for a maximum of **3 years** after the study concludes, then deleted
- Anonymised aggregate results may be published in a research paper; no individual will be identifiable
